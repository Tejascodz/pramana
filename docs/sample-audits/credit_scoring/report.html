Executive summary

Release is blocked: 7 high or critical risks need treatment before Nilgiri Finance Personal Loan Pre-Approval Model v2.3.0 goes live.

Controls over this AI system do not yet manage its key risks. Release should wait for remediation of high and critical findings. This system is high-risk under the EU AI Act, and its India context sensitivity is high. Next milestone: Digital Omnibus: new prohibited practices apply; end of Art. 50(2) marking grace period for systems already on the market (2026-12-02, 42 days).

What needs attention

  • highR-01 The documentation overstates or contradicts what the audit measured. Owner: Credit Risk Analytics
  • highR-02 Applicants in the 'rural' group (residence) receive a favourable outcome 68% of the time versus 94% for 'urban', below the four-fifths benchmark. Owner: Model Risk Management
  • highR-03 The combined group '18-25 × rural' is treated less favourably than any single attribute reveals. Owner: Model Risk Management
  • highR-04 Some inputs (e.g. 'pincode_risk_band') closely track 'residence', so the model can discriminate indirectly even though that attribute is excluded. Owner: Model Risk Management
  • highR-05 This is a high-risk AI use under the EU AI Act, with strict duties on testing, documentation and human oversight. Its owner had classified it lower, so required controls may be missing. Owner: Chief Risk Officer

What is working

  • passRisk scores can be read as real probabilities.
  • passThe model does not reveal who was in its training data beyond chance level.
  • passThe audit tests caught every defect deliberately planted to check them.

Decisions and actions required

  • Credit Risk Analytics: Complete the missing sections, correct documented figures to match validated results, and make the model card part of the release checklist (by 2026-11-20)
  • Model Risk Management: Investigate the drivers of the gap (see proxy-feature results), consider reweighing or threshold adjustment, and document a fairness rationale approved by the risk owner (by 2026-11-20)
  • Model Risk Management: Report subgroup metrics alongside single-attribute metrics and set an explicit subgroup tolerance (by 2026-11-20)
  • Model Risk Management: For each flagged feature, document a business justification, test model variants without it, and compare fairness and performance (by 2026-11-20)
  • Accepted risk R-12 (Data quality) expires 2027-03-31; re-approval needed from Head of Credit Risk.
