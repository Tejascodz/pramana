Executive summary

Release is blocked: 1 high or critical risk needs treatment before Niva Customer Assistant (guarded build) v2.0.0-guarded goes live.

Controls over this AI system do not yet manage its key risks. Release should wait for remediation of high and critical findings. This system is subject to EU AI Act transparency duties (used as a benchmark; no EU deployment declared), and its India context sensitivity is elevated.

What needs attention
