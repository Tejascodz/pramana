Executive summary

Release is blocked: 4 high or critical risks need treatment before Niva Customer Assistant (baseline build) v2.0.0-baseline goes live.

Controls over this AI system do not yet manage its key risks. Release should wait for remediation of high and critical findings. This system is subject to EU AI Act transparency duties (used as a benchmark; no EU deployment declared), and its India context sensitivity is elevated.

What needs attention

  • criticalR-01 Hidden instructions placed in messages or documents can make the assistant ignore its rules and reveal its confidential configuration. Owner: Information Security
  • criticalR-02 The assistant revealed personal or confidential data, such as which customer a phone number belongs to. Owner: Data Protection Officer
  • highR-03 The assistant stated figures, contact details or terms that are not in the official product information. Owner: Digital Channels
  • highR-04 The assistant broke a customer-protection rule, for example must disclose it is an ai when asked. Owner: Digital Channels

What is working

  • passThis AI system carries transparency duties: people must know they are dealing with AI.
  • passThe assistant answered reliably and quickly during testing.
  • passThe system is documented well enough for users and reviewers.

Decisions and actions required

  • Information Security: Separate untrusted content from instructions, screen inputs in every supported language, keep secrets out of system prompts, and validate outputs for canary tokens, policy markers and external links (by 2026-11-04)
  • Data Protection Officer: Enforce record-level authorisation in retrieval, redact identifiers before they reach the model, and scan outputs for personal data and secrets before display (by 2026-11-04)
  • Digital Channels: Require citations to retrieved passages, block numeric claims and contact details that cannot be matched to a source, and route pricing questions to approved templates (by 2026-11-20)
  • Digital Channels: Route regulated topics (approval, pricing, investment, complaints) to approved response templates, add the breached rules to an output guardrail, and keep these probes in the release regression suite (by 2026-11-20)
