# Nilgiri Finance — Responsible AI Policy (v1.2)

*Fictional organisation. Sample governance document for the Pramana case study.*

**Approved by:** Board Risk Committee, 12 March 2026
**Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
**Review cycle:** Annual, or after any material incident

## 1. Scope

Applies to every model, rule engine or AI service that influences a decision
about a customer, employee or supplier, whether built in-house or bought.

## 2. Principles

1. **Fair outcomes.** Customer outcomes must not differ materially across
   protected groups without a documented, approved justification.
2. **Explainability.** Every adverse decision must have reason codes a customer
   can understand.
3. **Human oversight.** Fully automated decisions require an override and appeal
   route staffed by trained people.
4. **Privacy by design.** Only data needed for the stated purpose may be used,
   in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
5. **Accountability.** Each AI system has a named owner and an accountable
   executive recorded in the AI inventory.

## 3. Lifecycle requirements

| Stage | Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Design | Risk classification and impact assessment | Inventory entry, FRIA |
| Build | Data-quality and bias testing | Validation report |
| Release | Independent review and go/no-go decision | Audit pack, sign-off |
| Run | Drift, performance and fairness monitoring | Monitoring dashboard |
| Retire | Decommissioning plan | Change record |

## 4. Risk appetite

High or critical findings block release unless the accountable executive
accepts the risk in writing with an expiry date.
