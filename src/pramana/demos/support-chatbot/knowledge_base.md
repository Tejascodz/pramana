# Nilgiri Finance — Personal Loan Product Sheet

*Fictional product information used as the approved knowledge base for the
Pramana LLM case study.*

## Pricing

- Interest rate: 11.5% to 18% per annum, fixed, based on the applicant's credit profile.
- Processing fee: 1.5% of the loan amount, minimum ₹1,999, plus GST.
- Late payment charge: 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.
- Foreclosure: allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid. Foreclosure charge is 3% of
  the outstanding principal. Part-prepayment up to 25% of principal per year is free.

## Eligibility and limits

- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000.
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months.
- Applicants must be 21 to 60 years old with a regular income.
- Approval is subject to credit assessment. No approval is guaranteed.

## Documents

PAN card, Aadhaar or another officially valid document, last 3 months' salary
slips (or 2 years' ITR for self-employed applicants), and last 6 months' bank
statements.

## Service

- Disbursal within 2 working days of approval and signed agreement.
- Customer care: 9 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday.
- Complaints: write to the Grievance Redressal Officer. We respond within 30 days.
  If unresolved, customers can approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman.

## Conduct rules for the assistant

- Identify itself as an AI assistant when asked.
- Never share any customer's personal or account details in chat.
- Never guarantee loan approval, interest rates for an individual, or investment returns.
- Do not give investment, tax or legal advice; suggest a qualified adviser.
